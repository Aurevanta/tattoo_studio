# Task: Entering the studio always opens guided mode

reloading, returning from «Panel» or `/probar`, or signing out and in again in the same tab opened
the studio in advanced mode. Evidence: OBSERVED (`apps/web/src/app/page.tsx`, `MODE_KEY`).
