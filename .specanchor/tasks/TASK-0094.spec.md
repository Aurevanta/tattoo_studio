# Task: Error pages in Spanish, and browser failures reach the Panel (audit 2026-10-08 UX-06, ARQ-02)

## 1. Problem / requested change

The audit of 2026-10-08, Sprint 0: "`not-found.tsx`, `error.tsx` y `global-error.tsx` en español,
con envío del error al Panel". The owner: "empieza". Evidence: INTENT.

## 2. Current behavior

There was no `app/not-found.tsx`, `app/error.tsx` or `app/global-error.tsx`. A wrong address
showed Next's English page (production: `<title>404: This page could not be found.</title>`),
and a screen that failed to render showed Next's generic error. No browser failure reached
anyone: the Panel's recent errors held only what the servers recorded. Evidence: VERIFIED
(`curl` on production and a search of `apps/web/src/app`, 2026-10-08).

## 3. Requirements

- **REQ-001** — A wrong address shows a Spanish page in the studio's card («Esta página no
  existe») with «Volver al estudio», and answers 404.
- **REQ-002** — A screen that fails to render shows a Spanish card with «Reintentar» and «Volver
  al estudio»; a failure of the root layout does the same with its own document.
- **REQ-003** — Such a failure is reported to `POST /api/client-errors`, which records a
  `client_error` event (operation `render` or `global`) for a signed-in account only: the message
  cut to 300 characters, the path without its query string, the digest; at most 20 per account
  and hour. It always answers 204.
- **REQ-004** — The worker accepts `client_error` from the web, and the Panel lists it under
  «Errores recientes» as «Error en el navegador».

## 4. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001** — `client-errors/route.test.ts`: recorded as the account with the path stripped of
  its query; nothing for a stranger (204); bounded fields and unknown places mapped to `render`;
  20 of 25 reports kept in an hour.
- **AC-002** — `test_studio.py::test_a_screen_that_failed_in_the_browser_reaches_recent_errors`:
  the event is accepted and `overview(...)["errors"]` lists it with its message; it fails
  without the worker change.
- **AC-003** — A production build served locally answers an unknown address with 404 and the
  Spanish card, and `POST /api/client-errors` without a session with 204.
