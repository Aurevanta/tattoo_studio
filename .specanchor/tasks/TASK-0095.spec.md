# Task: The client sees how many designs are left today (audit 2026-10-08 UX-04)

## 1. Problem / requested change

The audit of 2026-10-08, Sprint 0: "Contador de diseños restantes". The owner: "empieza".
Evidence: INTENT.

## 2. Current behavior

The daily limits of TASK-0078 (designs per account, designs for the whole studio, consultation
turns) were enforced by the worker, but the client learnt about them only when refused with 429,
sometimes halfway through a design. No route reported what was left. Evidence: OBSERVED
(`jobs/queue.py` `enqueue`; no quota read in `apps/web/src`).

## 3. Requirements

- **REQ-001** — `GET /studio/quota` answers, for the calling account, the designs and turns used,
  the limit and what remains today, and when the day resets (next UTC midnight). Designs left are
  the account's allowance within the studio's (`designs_left`), by the same counts `enqueue`
  applies; a re-placement is not counted.
- **REQ-002** — `GET /api/quota` passes it on for the signed-in account (401 for a stranger, 503
  for a malformed answer, `no-store`).
- **REQ-003** — The summary (step 4) shows «Te quedan N de M diseños hoy» (singular for 1). With
  nothing left it says why — the account's own allowance or the studio's — in the warning colour,
  and «Esto es lo que quiero» is disabled. The result's change request shows the same line and is
  disabled with nothing left. An unknown count never blocks: the worker still enforces the limit.
- **REQ-004** — The counter is read on load and whenever a design starts or ends.
