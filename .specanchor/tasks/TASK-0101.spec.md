# Task: Turn a lower-leg design round a reference leg in 3D (ADR-0033)

## 1. Problem / requested change

The owner asked: «¿cómo podría hacer para crear tatuajes envolventes? por ejemplo un tatuaje que
cubra toda la pierna izquierda desde la rodilla hasta abajo… ¿se podría generar una visión 3D donde
podamos rotar la imagen?» The owner then chose to start with the 3D viewer, on an anatomical
reference model. The wrap-around brief, the seamless 360° design and the sleeve stencil come later.
Evidence: INTENT (owner, 2026-10-14).

## 2. Current behavior

A design is shown only as a 2D mockup on one photograph of skin. That view covers at most the
visible half of a limb (`mockup/engine.py`, `MAX_SPILL`). There is no 3D view and no 3D dependency.
Evidence: OBSERVED.

## 3. Requirements

- **REQ-001** — The result of a design for the open consultation, in zone `calf`, `shin` or
  `ankle`, offers «Ver en 3D alrededor de la pierna».
  - The view shows the design's master on a reference lower leg.
  - It turns 360° and zooms within bounds.
  - It has buttons for front, outside, back and inside.
  - Controls move the design round the leg and up or down it.
  - The client chooses the left or right leg and one of three reference skin tones.
- **REQ-002** — The design keeps its real size in mm along the skin and is never redrawn. On the
  right leg it reads the right way round. It stays between 40 mm above the ankle joint and 30 mm
  below the knee. The view says how far round the leg the design reaches.
- **REQ-003** — The model is CC0. It is built reproducibly from the MakeHuman base mesh by a script
  in the repository, and its source and licence are recorded. No photograph is read, nothing is
  sent to a provider, and nothing is stored.
- **REQ-004** — The view carries «Vista orientativa sobre una pierna de referencia, no la tuya…»
  (PROD-INV-005). Without WebGL, or when the model or design fails to load, it says so and leaves
  nothing behind.
- **REQ-005** — three.js (pinned `0.186.1`) and the model load only when the view is opened.
  `/models/` is public like `/ejemplos/`. It works at 375 px without horizontal scroll, and the
  production CSP holds.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001** — The model matches an independent measurement and known shapes.
  - In `leg-wrap.test.ts`, the GLB carries its measurements, the CC0 licence, the MakeHuman source
    and the attributes `_RING`, `_PERIM` and `_HEIGHT`.
  - Every ring vector has unit length.
  - An independent slice of the mesh at the calf agrees with the recorded calf circumference
    within 0.5 %.
  - The build script's ring tables give the perimeter of a cylinder and a frustum within 0.5 %,
    and front, outside, back and inside fall at 0, 0.25, 0.5 and 0.75.
- **AC-002** — `leg-wrap.test.ts` checks placement and the starting position.
  - An 80 × 150 mm design reaches its edge at 40 mm of arc on a 344 mm leg and on a 204 mm leg.
  - The top of the design runs towards the knee.
  - Crossing the front leaves no seam.
  - On the right leg the design is mirrored back.
  - A calf piece starts at the back and a shin piece at the front.
  - The design stays between the clearances.
- **AC-003** — The repository checks pass, and three.js stays out of the main page chunk.
  - `pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck` and `pnpm test` all pass.
  - `next build` passes, with three.js absent from the studio page's chunk.
  - `middleware.test.ts` checks that `/models/lower-leg.glb` is not gated.
- **AC-004** — Checked in a browser with Playwright and Chromium.
  - `next start`: the model is served 200 `model/gltf-binary`, and no CSP violation is reported.
  - The four views are captured at 1280 × 800 and the page at 375 × 812, with no overflow.
  - A 344 × 300 mm lettered 1 cm chart wraps the leg fully, A–D in order.
  - On the right leg the letters are not mirrored.
  - With WebGL disabled the fallback text shows.
