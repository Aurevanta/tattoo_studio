# Task: Sleeves for the lower leg and the forearm (ADR-0034)

## 1. Problem / requested change

The owner: «¿cómo podría hacer para crear tatuajes envolventes? … que cubra toda la pierna
izquierda desde la rodilla hasta abajo». Then, for phase 2:

- Reference measurements only.
- An extra pass for the seam, counting as one more design.
- The lower leg and the forearm.

Evidence: INTENT, 2026-10-14.

## 2. Current behavior

- `orientation: "wrapping"` could be set, but nothing used it.
- The size of a design was at most a zone's front span.
- The prompt asked for an isolated motif.
- The 2D mockup covers about 180° of a limb.

Evidence: OBSERVED.

## 3. Requirements

- **REQ-001 (contracts)**: The reference anatomy (`body-zones.json`) gains `wraps`:
  - `lower_leg`: `calf` and `shin`, 344 × 310 mm.
  - `forearm`: `inner_forearm` and `outer_forearm`, 255 × 215 mm.
  - `wrapSize`, `wrapRegionOf` and `isSleeve` (TypeScript), and `tattoo_contracts.sleeve` (Python),
    apply one rule: wrapping, a zone in a region, and a width of at least 90 % of its circumference.
  - `studio-status.stage` gains `seam`.
  - All of this is additive (CONTRACTS-INV-004).
- **REQ-002 (consultation)**: The consultation reads a sleeve from the client's words:
  - Phrases: «manga», «de la rodilla al tobillo», «toda la pierna», «todo el antebrazo», «del codo a
    la muñeca», «que rodee/envuelva», «alrededor de la pantorrilla/del antebrazo».
  - It sets `orientation: wrapping`, the zone (the one named on that limb, otherwise the calf or the
    outer forearm) and the sleeve's size.
  - «Estilo/dibujos manga», «anime», the upper arm and a plain motif are never sleeves.
  - A later qualitative size and the size proposal do not replace a sleeve's size.
- **REQ-003 (generation)**: For a sleeve:
  - The artwork prompt adds `SLEEVE_DESIGN` (whole width, edges continuing), on a square canvas.
  - `repair_seam` rolls the design half a turn and sends it to the editor with a mask open down the
    middle. It keeps only the band from the answer and rolls the design back.
  - `generation.seam` measures the join, rolls exactly (also for an odd width), and fits the
    proportions without cutting the width.
  - Only generated artwork is sent; no photograph.
- **REQ-004 (platform: `app/studio.py`)**: A sleeve is drawn, cleared of backdrop, and has its seam
  repaired once, and again if the join still reads as a cut; it is then fitted to its proportions.
  - Its mockup is the middle half of the design laid over the zone's plate.
  - Its result carries the «Manga completa…» notice, which says when the join may still show.
  - The coverage controls refuse it with `SLEEVE_FIXED`.
  - The job reports the `seam` stage.
- **REQ-005 (jobs)**: `design_cost` counts a sleeve as 2, a design as 1 and a re-placement as 0.
  - The `billable` column holds that weight, and the limits sum it.
  - With one design left, a sleeve is refused with `SLEEVE_NEEDS_TWO`.
- **REQ-006 (web)**: Before a sleeve starts, the summary shows `sleeveNote`: it goes round, its size
  in cm, reference measurements, and that it counts as 2.
  - The result's 3D view starts a sleeve centred on the band.
  - The coverage controls are hidden for a sleeve.
  - Progress shows «Cerrando la vuelta de la manga…» as step 3 of 6, the same count for every design.
- **REQ-007 (mockup)**: `mockup.anatomy` re-exports the sleeve rule from the contracts, so the
  module graph gains no edge.
