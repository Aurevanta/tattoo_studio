# Task: A sleeve's stencil that follows the limb, in four panels (ADR-0035)

## 1. Problem / requested change

The owner asked for phase 3, the sleeve stencil. Before building it, the geometry review found a
problem: a sleeve was a rectangle as wide as the widest circumference (34.4 cm). On a leg that is
20.4 cm round above the ankle, the 3D view cut off the excess, and the flat stencil could not be
laid round the leg.

The owner chose (INTENT, 2026-10-15):

- four panels;
- the same rule for the stencil, the 3D view and the mockup.

## 2. Current behavior

- `export_svg`/`export_pdf` print the sleeve's rectangle at 1:1.
- The shader maps every design in millimetres across the skin and clips what overflows a narrow
  row.
- Nothing produces panels.

Evidence: OBSERVED.

## 3. Requirements

- **REQ-001 (contracts)**: Each `wraps` region in `body-zones.json` gains three additive fields:
  - `circumferenceProfileMm`, every `profileStepMm` (10 mm) down its band, measured on the
    reference models.
  - `views` (the names of the limb's sides).
  - `zoneAround` (each zone's side).

  `circumferenceAt` (TypeScript) and `WrapRegion.circumference_at` / `side` (Python) agree.
  `studio-status.artifact` and `shared-design` gain optional `panels` and `panelsMirror` assets.
- **REQ-002 (stencil)**: `stencil.sleeve.unroll` lays every row of the master at its height's
  circumference, centred, with strokes densified.
  - `panels` gives four quarter turns, starting on the zone's own side and mirrored for the right
    side.
  - Each panel overlaps its neighbours by at least 10 mm and carries the neighbours' lines, the
    centre line, ticks numbered «n·k» every 50 mm, and its name.
  - `export_panels_pdf` prints each panel at 1:1 and then in A4 pieces, in normal and mirrored
    versions.
- **REQ-003 (platform)**: A sleeve's `stencil`, `stencilMirror`, `pdf` and `pdfMirror` are the
  unrolled skin, and its result carries `panels` and `panelsMirror`. Any other design is unchanged.
  The tattooer's link shares the panels when present.
- **REQ-004 (web)**:
  - The 3D view maps a sleeve by its share of the turn (`LimbPlacement.wraps`), in the shader and
    in `designCoordinate`.
  - Its note says the design goes the whole way round at that height.
  - The result offers «Manga en 4 paneles (PDF)» and its mirror, with an explanation.
  - The tattooer's page offers them.
  - The limb sides in `LIMBS` equal the contract's `views`.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001 (contracts)**: `test_sleeve.py` and `corpus.test.ts` check the profile's length, the
  interpolation (clamped at both ends), widest equal to the circumference, the ankle narrower,
  the side names and `zoneAround`. A `shared-design` fixture `sleeve-panels` is valid in both
  runtimes.
- **AC-002 (stencil)**: `stencil/tests/test_sleeve.py` checks that:
  - each row unrolls to its circumference, centred, on the leg and the forearm;
  - a long stroke bends with the limb;
  - the panels' names follow the zone and the side, with the right neighbours;
  - every row is covered with at least 10 mm of overlap;
  - a motif on a panel edge appears in both panels, and one across the design's edges appears in
    the panel opposite the zone;
  - the guides and ticks are right;
  - the PDF has 4 × (1 + A4 pieces) pages, the panel names and the instruction, and its mirror
    differs.
- **AC-003 (platform)**: `app/tests/test_sleeve.py` checks that:
  - a sleeve's result carries the panels PDF, named;
  - its SVG stays within the widest circumference;
  - the shared view validates and opens the panels;
  - an ordinary design has no panels.
- **AC-004 (web)**: `limb-wrap.test.ts` checks the share-of-turn mapping on a wide and a narrow
  row, the right side, nothing cut off, and the side names equal the contract's.
  `SharedDesignView.test.tsx` checks the panels links. In a browser, a sleeve-size chart closes the
  turn at every height, wider at the calf and narrower at the ankle.
- **AC-005**: The checks pass:
  - `pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck` and `pnpm test`, and `next build`;
  - pnpm 12 frozen install;
  - the contracts' and the worker's ruff, format, mypy and pytest;
  - codegen in sync.
