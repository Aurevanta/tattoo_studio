# Task: Edit the mockup live: move, size, stretch and turn (ADR-0036)

## 1. Problem / requested change

The owner wants to edit the mockup in real time from the studio's result: make the tattoo larger or
smaller, wider or taller, move it over the zone and turn it, and see the result at once (INTENT,
2026-10-15). The decisions are recorded in ADR-0036:

- saving creates a version with the AI finish;
- the stencil and the PDFs follow the millimetres;
- stretch is limited to ±25 %;
- rotation is free, ±180°;
- the editor replaces «Más pequeño» and «Más grande».

## 2. Current behavior

- The result shows a pre-composed PNG.
- «Más pequeño» and «Más grande» each run a job that changes only the view.
- Only «Ocupar toda la zona» changes the millimetres, and only uniformly.
- The worker cannot rotate a design or place it exactly where it is told.

Evidence: OBSERVED (`TattooPreviewModal.tsx`, `app/studio.py::reposition`, `mockup/engine.py`).

## 3. Requirements

- **REQ-001 (contracts)**: Both changes are additive and optional.
  - `studio-job.edit.placement` is `{centreX, centreY, width, height, angle}`, with centres in
    [0, 1], width and height in (0, 2], and angle in [-180, 180].
  - The same object is allowed in `studio-status`'s `edit`. `studio-status.transform` gains
    `placement` (same shape) and `stretch` (0.75–1.25).
- **REQ-002 (mockup)**:
  - `composite(..., manual=True)` places the design with its centre at `centreX` and `centreY`, at
    `width × frame width` by `height × frame height` before rotation.
  - It turns the design by `angle` (clockwise) before the cylinder warp.
  - It clips to the frame and to the silhouette, without re-centring or shrinking.
  - It records `placement` in the transform.
  - Every other call is unchanged.
- **REQ-003 (stencil)**: `rescale_axes(master, width_mm, height_mm)` scales a vector master per
  axis.
  - The stroke scales by the geometric mean of the two factors.
  - At equal factors its paths equal `rescale`'s.
- **REQ-004 (platform)**: An edit with `placement` is a re-placement.
  - It derives the millimetres from the parent's placement.
  - It refuses a cumulative stretch outside [0.75, 1.25], and a size or stretch change on a
    calibrated photo.
  - When the millimetres change, it rescales the vector per axis and re-exports SVG, PDF and the
    mirrors. If the stretch changes, it also resamples the raster master.
  - It runs the finish graph and writes a notice with the millimetres and the angle.
  - It refuses sleeves.
  - A parent without a vector keeps its millimetres, and only its position and angle change.
- **REQ-005 (web)**:
  - «Ajustar sobre la piel» in the result composes `master` over `background` live.
    - It supports drag, plus sliders for size, width, height and angle, and a reset.
    - It shows «≈ W × H mm» and the stretch, and warns when the drawing is deformed.
  - Saving with new millimetres asks for confirmation, then sends `edit.placement`.
  - «Más pequeño» and «Más grande» are removed.
  - The editor is absent for sleeves, camera versions, results without `background`, and
    calibrated photos (where it offers position and angle only).
  - It makes no request while adjusting (ADR-0019).

## 4. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001**: The contract corpus accepts a placement edit and the new transform fields, and
  rejects an angle of 200 and a stretch of 1.4, in both runtimes.
- **AC-002 (mockup)**: Worker tests check that:
  - a manual composite centres the design where it is told;
  - it rotates the ink (a vertical bar becomes horizontal at 90°);
  - a stretched placement gets the asked aspect;
  - a placement half off the frame is clipped rather than refused.
- **AC-003**: `rescale_axes` doubles the width and keeps the height. At equal factors it equals
  `rescale`.
- **AC-004 (platform)**: Worker tests check that:
  - a placement edit yields new millimetres proportional to the change, a re-exported stencil at
    those millimetres, and `transform.placement` and `stretch`;
  - a stretch beyond 1.25 is refused;
  - a sleeve is refused.
- **AC-005 (web)**: `mockup-editor.test.ts` checks:
  - the initial placement taken from the transform;
  - the millimetre and stretch arithmetic;
  - the stretch clamp;
  - the cylinder's source mapping, which matches the worker's at sample points;
  - a source scan finding no network call.
- **AC-006**: In a browser with the dev server, dragging and the sliders move, resize, stretch and
  turn the design live in the result.
- **AC-007**: `pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm test` and worker ruff, mypy and pytest pass.
  Contracts codegen is in sync.

## 8. What changes in the printable stencil

- Saving a larger, smaller, wider or taller design changes the brief's millimetres to what the
  editor showed («≈ W × H mm»). The SVG, the 1:1 PDF and their mirrors are re-exported at that
  size from the vector master, scaled exactly (per axis when stretched, ±25 % at most).
- A rotation is **not** printed: the stencil stays upright, and the notice tells the tattooer the
  angle to lay it at.
- Moving the design changes nothing that is printed.
- The A4 pieces follow the new size, as they already did for «Ocupar toda la zona».
