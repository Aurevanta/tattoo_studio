# Task: The zone comes with the design, so the 3D view always opens

## 1. Problem / requested change

The owner opened a lower-leg design (195.8 × 425 mm) and found no «Ver en 3D» button. They asked
for the zone to come with the design, so the 3D view always opens (INTENT, owner, 2026-10-15).

## 2. Current behavior

- A result does not carry its zone.
- `page.tsx` read it from the open consultation, and only when `artifact.briefId` equalled the
  session. So a design opened from the history of another consultation had no 3D view, and no zone
  span for «Ocupar toda la zona».

Evidence: OBSERVED (`page.tsx` `limbView` and `zoneSpan`, TASK-0102, TASK-0073).

## 3. Requirements

- **REQ-001 (contracts)**: `studio-status` gains an optional `placement` on the artifact:
  `{bodyPart, orientation, side?}`. This is where the design goes and nothing else from the brief.
  The change is additive.
- **REQ-002 (jobs)**: `JobQueue.public` adds that placement to every result it serves, old
  ones included. It reads it from the job's stored brief (`body_zone`). A payload without a zone
  serves the result unchanged.
- **REQ-003 (web)**: `lib/design-zone.ts` resolves the zone from the design itself.
  - It falls back to the open consultation only for that consultation's own designs.
  - The 3D view (`limbViewOf`) and the zone span (`zoneSpanOf`) use it.
  - The editor's sleeve check (ADR-0036) follows, since it reads `limbView`.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001**: The fixtures `with-zone` (valid) and `zone-with-body-type` (invalid) agree in both
  runtimes.
- **AC-002**: A worker test checks that `get` and `history` serve the brief's zone, and that
  `body_zone` refuses non-JSON and a zone without orientation.
- **AC-003**: `design-zone.test.ts` checks:
  - the design's zone wins over another consultation;
  - the fallback applies only to that consultation's own designs;
  - the 3D view opens for a lower leg on its side, as a sleeve when it wraps, and not for a chest.
- **AC-004**: lint, typecheck and the TypeScript and Python suites pass.
