# Task: Every tattooable zone can be seen in 3D, on one reference body (ADR-0037)

## 1. Problem / requested change

The 3D view covered only the lower leg and the forearm. The owner asked for it everywhere a tattoo
can go (INTENT, 2026-10-15). They chose one neutral reference body from the same CC0 base mesh,
delivered in one task.

## 2. Current behavior

`LimbViewer3D` served five zones (calf, shin, ankle, inner and outer forearm). The other 21
`bodyPart` zones had no 3D view. Evidence: OBSERVED (`lib/limb-wrap.ts::ZONE_LIMB`).

## 3. Requirements

- **REQ-001**: `scripts/build-body-model.py` builds `public/models/body.glb` from `base.obj`
  (SHA-256 `8e761e66…f6fb4c`, CC0).
  - It uses the `body` group with one Catmull-Clark step, in mm, with the floor at 0.
  - Its scene extras name the source and the licence, and give each of the 21 zones its
    projection (`plane` or `cylinder`), axes, skin point, depth slab or radius, side pairing and
    complexity.
- **REQ-002**: `lib/body-zones.ts` holds the pure maths.
  - `designCoordinate` is the shader twin: plane decal with a depth slab and a facing test, or arc
    round an axis at the skin's own radius.
  - It handles offset, clockwise turn and mirror.
  - `isBodyZone` covers exactly the zones the limb view does not.
- **REQ-003**: `lib/body-scene.ts` and `BodyViewer3D` draw the design at its real size on the
  zone.
  - The client can orbit, look at the zone or at the whole body, move the design sideways and up
    or down, turn it, choose the side (for paired zones) and choose the tone.
  - The view carries the ADR-0037 notice, plus the complexity warning on the neck, behind the ear,
    the hand, the foot and the finger.
  - It loads on demand and sends nothing.
- **REQ-004**: The result offers «Ver en 3D sobre …» for every body zone (`bodyViewOf`), and keeps
  the limb view for its five zones.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001**: `body-zones.test.ts` checks:
  - the 26 brief zones split 5 / 21 between the two views;
  - the committed GLB carries the 21 zones with unit axes, its source and the CC0 licence;
  - the decal's mm mapping, the slab and the facing test;
  - offset, turn and mirror;
  - the cylinder's arc length;
  - the view distance bounds.
- **AC-002**: `design-zone.test.ts` checks `bodyViewOf`: sides, a centred zone, and a limb zone
  excluded.
- **AC-003**: In a browser, every one of the 21 zones shows the design on the right place of the
  body, lit and unoccluded.
- **AC-004**: lint, typecheck and `pnpm test` pass. Two builds of the model are byte-identical.
