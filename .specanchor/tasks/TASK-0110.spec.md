# Task: A masculine and a feminine body in every 3D view (ADR-0038)

## 1. Problem / requested change

The owner found the neutral body plain and asked for a masculine and a feminine one, chosen by the
design's body (INTENT, 2026-10-15). They decided on a «Hombre / Mujer» toggle, on masculine and
feminine limbs too, and on no clothing.

## 2. Current behavior

- One neutral body (ADR-0037) and one neutral lower leg and forearm (ADR-0033).
- A sleeve's stencil is unrolled on the neutral limb's profile (ADR-0035).
- A result's zone carries no body (TASK-0108). Its schema even refuses `bodyType`.

Evidence: OBSERVED.

## 3. Requirements

- **REQ-001 (models)**: `build-limb-model.py` and `build-body-model.py` take a targets directory
  and a body.
  - `apply_body` adds MakeHuman's three race targets of that sex, young, at one third each.
  - A body's limb keeps the reference length, stretched axially above the distal joint only.
  - The body model's anchors are placed on the neutral base and carried with the skin.
  - Six GLBs are committed. `body.glb` is removed.
  - `measure-limb-profile.py` measures a band profile.
- **REQ-002 (contracts)**: `body-zones.json` wraps gain `bodies.{masculine,feminine}.
  circumferenceProfileMm`. `circumferenceAt(region, depth, bodyType?)` (TS) and
  `WrapRegion.circumference_at(depth, body_type)` and `for_body` (Python) read them. The
  artifact's `placement` admits `bodyType`, and the fixtures follow.
- **REQ-003 (generation, platform, jobs)**:
  - `plate_library.plate_body` is the plate's body rule.
  - `design_body` and `design_zone` name the body of a design: the plate's, or none on an own
    photo.
  - The result carries `placement` with it. A sleeve's stencil is unrolled on `for_body` of it.
  - `body_zone` passes a stored brief's `bodyType` through.
- **REQ-004 (web)**:
  - `lib/body-type.ts` gives the labels, the guard and `defaultBody(briefId)`, the twin of
    `plate_body`.
  - `limbModelUrl` and `bodyModelUrl` name the models.
  - `LimbViewer3D` and `BodyViewer3D` take a body and offer «Hombre / Mujer». Switching rebuilds
    the scene and keeps the placement, side and tone.
  - The result passes the design's body, or the default one.
  - The landing keeps the neutral leg, without the toggle.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001**: Contract tests (TS and Python) check that the profiles have the band's sample count,
  that the named body's profile is read and the neutral one otherwise, and that the feminine
  maximum is below the masculine. `for_body` keeps the length and returns the region itself
  without a body.
- **AC-002**: Worker tests check:
  - a sleeve unrolls to the named body's circumference;
  - `design_body` and `design_zone` handle a named body, a hashed one and an own photo;
  - a result carries the plate's body.
- **AC-003**: Web tests check:
  - `defaultBody` matches Python's `sha256(...)[0] % 2` on two inputs;
  - each model names its body, and the limbs keep the neutral `proximalMm`;
  - `bodyViewOf` and `limbViewOf` pass the body;
  - each body GLB carries 21 zones.
- **AC-004**: In a browser, both bodies and both limbs render with the design, and the toggle
  loads the other model.
- **AC-005**: Lint, typecheck and every suite pass. Every model rebuilds byte-identically.
