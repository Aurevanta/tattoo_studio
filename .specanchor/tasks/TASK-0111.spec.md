# Task: The 3D views show the design at the mockup's size and place

## 1. Problem / requested change

The owner opened a thigh design in 3D and found it smaller than in the 2D mockup, and not where
the mockup shows it. They asked for the same size, or a similar size and position (INTENT,
2026-10-15).

## 2. Current behavior

- **Size:** the 3D views (ADR-0033, ADR-0037) draw the master's whole canvas, white margin
  included, at the brief's millimetres. The 2D mockup trims the margin (`visible_artwork`) and
  gives the visible artwork the brief's height (`visible_size`). A design with a wide margin is
  therefore smaller in 3D.
- **Place:** the 3D views always start at the zone's centre, upright.

Evidence: OBSERVED (`body-scene.ts`, `limb-scene.ts`, `mockup/engine.py`).

## 3. Requirements

- **REQ-001 (size)**: Both 3D views draw the artwork as the mockup does.
  - `lib/design-art.ts::loadArtwork` trims the master with the same rule as `trimArtwork`.
  - `lib/mockup-pose.ts::visibleSize` gives the visible artwork the brief's height.
  - A sleeve and a calibrated photograph keep the whole canvas, as the mockup does there.
  - The scenes take the artwork as a canvas.
- **REQ-002 (place)**: On a generated plate, the 3D views start where the mockup shows the design.
  - `mockupOffset` reads the mockup's placement: `initialPlacement` on the background's worker
    frame, narrowed to the visible artwork.
  - It turns that placement into millimetres from the zone's centre, with the plate's framing of
    the zone (`SKIN_FRAME_FRACTION` of the frame is the zone's reference height). It keeps the
    angle.
  - The body view applies x, y and the angle, clamped to its range. The limb view applies the
    height.
  - A later switch of body keeps where the client moved the design.
  - An own photograph or a camera version gives no pose.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001**: `mockup-pose.test.ts` checks the visible size with and without the whole canvas, no
  offset for a centred design, a tenth of the frame for a moved one, and the angle of a saved
  placement.
- **AC-002**: In a browser, a worker-composed thigh mockup and the 3D view show the Cronos design
  at a similar size, at a similar height and turned alike.
- **AC-003**: Lint, typecheck and tests pass.
