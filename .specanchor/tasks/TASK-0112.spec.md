# Task: The 3D views have the mockup editor's controls, and save to the mockup

## 1. Problem / requested change

The owner could not resize the tattoo in 3D. They asked for the same controls as the mockup
editor, and for changes made in 3D to apply to the mockup (INTENT, 2026-10-15).

## 2. Current behavior

- **Body view:** it moves the design sideways and up or down, and turns it.
- **Limb view:** it moves the design round the limb and up or down, and does not turn it.
- **Both:** neither changes the size, and neither saves.
- **The mockup editor (ADR-0036):** it has size, width, height (±25 % stretch), move and turn, and
  it saves `edit.placement`.

Evidence: OBSERVED (`BodyViewer3D.tsx`, `LimbViewer3D.tsx`, `MockupEditor.tsx`).

## 3. Requirements

- **REQ-001**: `lib/design-resize.ts` holds the editor's size rules in factors against the opening
  size:
  - the print size, rounded as the worker rounds it;
  - a uniform scale within 5–600 mm;
  - one axis within the ±25 % stretch, counting `transform.stretch`.

  `DesignAdjust3D` shows size, width and height, «≈ W × H mm» and the stretch warning. A calibrated
  photograph shows none.
- **REQ-002**: The limb view turns the design.
  - A `LimbPlacement.angle` is applied in the shader, with the screen gradients turned alike.
  - `designCoordinate` does the same in TypeScript.
  - A sleeve does not turn or resize.
- **REQ-003**: «Guardar esta colocación en el mockup» saves the 3D adjustment as the mockup's
  `edit.placement` (ADR-0036). `toMockupPlacement` is the inverse of `mockupOffset`:
  - the offset becomes the centre on the plate;
  - the factors scale the mockup's own width and height;
  - the turn is kept;
  - a limb keeps the mockup's centre across.

  Saving new millimetres asks for confirmation. Save is offered where the 2D editor is (not on a
  sleeve, a camera version or without a skin) and only with a generated plate's pose.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001**: `design-resize.test.ts` checks the print size, the 5–600 mm bound and the
  accumulated 25 % stretch.
- **AC-002**: `limb-wrap.test.ts` checks that a quarter turn lays the design on its side and points
  its top to the right.
- **AC-003**: `mockup-pose.test.ts` checks that `toMockupPlacement` inverts `mockupOffset` within a
  millimetre and carries the move, the turn, the size, and a limb's kept centre.
- **AC-004**: In a browser, the 3D view resizes, moves and turns the design. Saving gives a
  placement whose worker render and millimetres match what the 3D view showed.
- **AC-005**: Lint, typecheck and tests pass.
