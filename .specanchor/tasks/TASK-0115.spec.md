# Task: A daily limit on uploaded images, in place of ten per account for life

## 1. Problem / requested change

The owner, uploading images to the idea, got «Límite de diez imágenes por cuenta. Elimina tus datos
para empezar de nuevo.» Since TASK-0113 an idea carries up to five images, so the limit runs out in
two ideas and only erasing everything lifts it. Offered a daily limit, a higher lifetime cap or
counting only live images, the owner chose the daily limit (INTENT, 2026-10-16).

## 2. Current behavior

`Studio._ingest` counted the account's assets that are not artifacts (references, own-body photos,
kept camera photos) and refused the eleventh, for life, after the moderation call. Evidence: OBSERVED
(`app/studio.py`, ADR-0022).

## 3. Requirements

- **REQ-001 (jobs)**: `Limits.uploads_per_account` (default 30) and `UploadLedger` count uploads per
  account per UTC day, like consultation turns. The ledger keeps only owner and time, prunes rows
  older than the previous day, and is not reset by erasing the account's data.
- **REQ-002 (platform)**: `Studio.ingest` reserves an upload before the moderation call; the lifetime
  count is removed. `POST /studio/media` answers 429 with «Has llegado al límite de N imágenes
  subidas por día…». Setting `TATTOO_DAILY_UPLOADS`, passed by the compose file.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001**: `test_uploads_have_a_daily_limit_not_a_lifetime_one`: twelve uploads pass with a limit
  of 12, the thirteenth is refused with the daily message, another account is unaffected, and
  erasing the data does not lift the day's limit.
- **AC-002**: `test_the_upload_ledger_starts_again_each_day_and_forgets_old_days`.
- **AC-003**: worker ruff, format, mypy and pytest pass; the guard baseline passes.
