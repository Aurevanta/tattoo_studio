# Task: The 3D view stays in sight while its controls are used

## Problem

The owner, with a screenshot of the 3D view on the upper back: «cuando edito el modelo 3D, tengo que
hacer scroll hacia abajo para poder ver las opciones y modificarlas, pero pierdo la vista del modelo
y no sé cómo estoy modificando hasta que no vuelvo a hacer scroll up» (INTENT, 2026-10-16). The
stage was `min(70vh, 620px)` tall with the TASK-0112 controls stacked below it, inside the result
dialog, which is what scrolls. Evidence: OBSERVED (`globals.css` `.leg-stage`, `BodyViewer3D.tsx`,
`LimbViewer3D.tsx`).

## Change

CSS only, for both 3D views (`.leg-viewer`, shared by the body and the limb):

- The stage is `position: sticky` at the top of the dialog (over its 28 px padding, so content
  scrolling by does not show above it).
- From 900 px wide, the view is on the left (3 : 2, `min(78vh, 620px)` tall) and the notices and
  controls on the right.
- Below 900 px, the stage stays on top, `min(42vh, 400px)` tall, and the controls scroll under it.

No markup, behaviour, contract or saved value changes.
