# Task: The mockup editor keeps the skin in sight, and the 3D view opens with the result

## Problem

After TASK-0117 shipped, the owner: «funciona, me gusta, aplica lo mismo para editar el tatuaje en
el mockup, que quede a la derecha las barras de edición como en el 3D, y que la vista en 3D esté
activa por defecto» (INTENT, 2026-10-16). The mockup editor (TASK-0107, ADR-0036) stacked its
canvas above its sliders, and the 3D view started hidden behind «Ver en 3D …». Evidence: OBSERVED
(`MockupEditor.tsx`, `TattooPreviewModal.tsx` `useState(false)`).

## Change

- **CSS** (`globals.css`): `.mockup-editor-stage` is sticky at the top of the result dialog, as the
  3D stage is. From 900 px it sits on the left (3 : 2, `min(78vh, 620px)`) and every other part of
  the editor — the note, the sliders in one column, the size, the notes and the actions — on the
  right. Below 900 px it stays on top, at most 42vh. The sliders take the 3D view's gold accent.
- **Behaviour** (`TattooPreviewModal.tsx`): `show3d` starts `true`, so the 3D view (body or limb)
  opens with the result; «Ocultar la vista 3D» still hides it. That is the one behavioural change:
  the 3D model is now loaded whenever a result with a 3D zone is opened.

## Acceptance

- **AC-001**: opening a result on the upper back shows «Ocultar la vista 3D» and the 3D stage, with
  no click.
- **AC-002**: at desktop width the editor's skin is on the left and its sliders on the right;
  scrolled to «Guardar esta colocación», the skin is still in view; dragging «Tamaño» to 250 %
  enlarges the design on it.
- **AC-003**: at 375 px, with «Guardar esta colocación» at the bottom, the skin is at the dialog's
  top edge (341 px tall), no horizontal scroll; the sliders are gold.
- **AC-004**: typecheck, eslint, prettier and vitest pass.
