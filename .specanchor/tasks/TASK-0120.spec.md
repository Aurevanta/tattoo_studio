# Task: The mockup and the 3D views show a design at the same place and size

## 1. Problem / requested change

The owner, with two screenshots of their latest design: «la representación 3D no tiene la misma
posición ni tamaño que el mockup … el tatuaje lo tengo con un tamaño determinado en el omóplato
derecho, pero la representación 3D me lo saca centrado y más grande … piensa en una solución
definitiva, se ha de representar igual en mockup y 3D en la primera propuesta y en las
modificaciones posteriores; si modifico mockup se traslada la misma modificación a 3D y viceversa».
Decisions asked and answered (INTENT, 2026-10-16): real millimetres rule; plates calibrated
automatically and reviewed; live sync; older designs keep their stencil. Decision: ADR-0042.

## 2. Current behavior (OBSERVED, at da95542)

- `lib/mockup-pose.ts::mockupOffset` turned the mockup's placement into millimetres assuming the
  plate frames its zone at `SKIN_FRAME_FRACTION` (82 %) of its height, centred.
- The masculine upper-back plate frames about 650 mm (measured, ev-002) against the 366 mm the rule
  assumed (300 mm / 0.82): offsets read from the mockup were about halved in 3D, and the mockup drew
  sizes on the same wrong scale. `fit_coverage` and `_fit_to_body` (TASK-0039) also re-centred and
  shrank first proposals on the silhouette.
- The mockup editor and the 3D view each kept their own placement; one followed the other only
  after a save created a version.

## 3. Requirements

- **REQ-001 (generation, platform)**: every library plate has a reviewed frame in millimetres
  (`generation/plates/calibration.json`, built and reviewed with `app.build_plate_calibration`);
  plates the review left on the convention are not listed. `PlateLibrary.frame(brief)` reads it,
  mirrored for a left side.
- **REQ-002 (mockup, platform)**: `convention_frame`, `frame_placement`; `parent_placement` and
  `manual_layout` start from the printed millimetres on a skin with a frame.
- **REQ-003 (platform)**: `Studio.skin_frame` / `describe`; the first proposal, a redraw and a
  whole-zone fill are laid at their millimetres (a redraw where its parent was), drawn as placed
  (`manual=True`), and a manual placement's millimetres are computed from the same start.
- **REQ-004 (jobs)**: `JobQueue(describe=…)`: a served result gains what `describe` returns
  (`skinFrame`), recomputed on every read; a stored `skinFrame` is dropped first.
- **REQ-005 (contracts)**: `studio-status` artifact gains an optional `skinFrame {widthMm, heightMm,
  centreX, centreY, calibrated}`. Additive.
- **REQ-006 (web)**: `initialPlacement` (editor), `mockupStart`, `offsetOf`, `mockupOffset` and
  `toMockupPlacement` use `skinFrame` when present, the convention otherwise.
- **REQ-007 (web)**: the result view shares one placement between the mockup editor and the 3D view
  (`TattooPreviewModal` `shared`); each follows the other live; a limb view shares height, turn and
  size, not its position round the limb.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001**: every plate is calibrated or deliberately on the convention; the masculine upper back
  frames more than 600 mm (`test_every_plate_is_calibrated_or_reviewed_onto_the_convention`); a
  left-side plate's frame is mirrored (`test_a_left_side_plate_mirrors_its_frame`).
- **AC-002**: a new design on a calibrated plate is drawn at its visible millimetres on the zone's
  centre and served with `skinFrame` (`test_a_new_design_is_laid_at_its_millimetres_on_the_zone`).
- **AC-003**: a placement moved 0.12 of the picture and halved prints at 140 mm and sits 0.12 of the
  frame's millimetres right; a redraw asked from it is drawn where it was
  (`test_a_moved_and_resized_design_keeps_the_view_and_the_print_in_step`).
- **AC-004**: a result composed without a frame starts at its printed millimetres
  (`test_a_design_made_before_calibration_starts_at_its_printed_size`; `mockup-pose.test.ts`).
- **AC-005**: web arithmetic: start, offset from the calibrated centre, 3D↔editor round trip, the
  limb's kept centre, the convention fallback (`mockup-pose.test.ts`, 5 new).
- **AC-006**: in a browser, a new upper-back design shows at the same relative size and place in the
  editor and in 3D; moving and resizing in 3D moves the editor, and turning in the editor turns 3D.

## 5. Decision log

- **DEC-001**: the frame is served on read, not stored, so recalibrating a plate corrects every
  version on it, and older versions get it without a migration.
- **DEC-002**: the shared state is the mockup editor's placement (fractions of the skin), because
  that is what a save sends and what the worker validates; the 3D views convert it.
- **DEC-003**: the reference body's width through a zone was unusable for upper arms, shoulders and
  extremities (a hanging arm joins the slice); those plates use a reviewed row and an adult
  reference width, or the convention where the plate frames the zone tightly.
