# Task: The 3D view waits for the skin before it opens, so it starts where the mockup is

## Problem

After TASK-0120 was deployed (547e9ba, confirmed by the owner in Coolify), «sigue posicionando mal
el 3D» on a design made before that day. Cause (INFERRED from the code, consistent with the
report): the 3D views read where the mockup has the design once, when their scene is built, from
`pose`, which needs the skin's pixel size (`mockupFrame`), loaded from the background image. Until
TASK-0118 the 3D view opened on a click, after the image had loaded; since TASK-0118 it opens with
the result, and on the deployed studio the background arrives after the scene is built, so the view
opened at its default: centred, at the zone's centre. Locally the image arrived first, which is why
TASK-0118 and TASK-0120's browser checks did not show it.

## Change

`TattooPreviewModal.tsx`: `frameSettled` is set once the background has loaded, failed, or there is
none; the 3D views (body and limb) are mounted only then, with «Cargando la vista 3D…» meanwhile.

## Acceptance

- **AC-001**: in a browser, a design moved in the mockup editor before TASK-0120 (centre 0.71, 0.47
  of an upper-back plate) opens in 3D at «Mover a los lados: 9 cm», «Subir o bajar: -3 cm» — the
  calibrated offset (0.21 × 432.9 mm, -0.04 × 649.4 mm) — and the editor and the 3D view both show
  ≈ 108 × 168 mm on the right shoulder blade.
- **AC-002**: typecheck, eslint, prettier and vitest pass.
