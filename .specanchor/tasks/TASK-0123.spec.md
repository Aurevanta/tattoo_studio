# Task: One size — every size change is a print change, confirmed, and every view follows it

## 1. Current behaviour (OBSERVED, c87235f)

- The live editors (2D and 3D) and «Ocupar toda la zona» change the printed millimetres after a
  confirmation naming them; stencil, PDFs and 3D follow (TASK-0107, TASK-0073, ADR-0042).
- A nudge («hazlo más grande», `coverage: larger|smaller`) scaled the mockup's projection by
  1.35 / 0.75 and kept the millimetres (`fit_coverage`, ADR-0008). The 3D view draws at the
  millimetres (ADR-0042), so the mockup and the 3D view and stencil disagreed. ADR-0042 recorded it.
- A whole-zone phrase inside a redraw filled the zone on the mockup at the old millimetres.

## 2. Desired behaviour (INTENT, owner's audit list 2026-10-16, item 1)

«"Más grande", los controles del editor y "Ocupar toda la zona" deben actualizar de forma coherente
mockup, 3D y plantilla, confirmando las nuevas medidas.» Decided in ADR-0043.

- **REQ-001**: a nudge resizes the print by ×1.25 / ×0.8 (whole mm, half up, 5–600 mm), rescales the
  vector exactly, re-exports SVG/PDF and mirrors, and the mockup is laid at the new millimetres
  (where the design was, on a plate with a frame). No provider call, no redraw.
- **REQ-002**: the result view confirms a written nudge with the current and new millimetres before
  sending it; the worker refuses a written nudge without the control, and a size control mixed with
  attached photos.
- **REQ-003**: a size phrase inside a redraw keeps the millimetres and the redraw is laid at them.

Out of scope: restoring the «Más grande» buttons (ADR-0036); designs without a stored vector
(refused, not enlarged on the picture).

## 3. Acceptance criteria

- **AC-001** (REQ-001, REQ-002): `test_a_nudge_resizes_the_print_and_the_view_together` — 80 × 150 →
  100 × 188 mm, stencil/PDFs re-exported at it, master reused, projection ×1.25 (±3 %), one provider
  call (the parent's); «más pequeño» returns within 1 mm; a written nudge → 422; size control with
  attachments → 422.
- **AC-002** (REQ-001): `test_a_nudge_beyond_what_can_be_tattooed_is_refused` and
  `size-step.test.ts` give the same sizes and refusals in both runtimes.
- **AC-003** (REQ-001): `test_a_nudge_on_a_calibrated_plate_grows_where_it_was` — on a calibrated
  upper-back plate 180 × 280 → 225 × 350 mm; `transform.placement` × skin frame equals the visible
  millimetres (±1 %) and its centre is the parent's (±0.002).
- **AC-004** (REQ-003): `test_a_size_phrase_in_a_redraw_keeps_the_millimetres_in_every_view`.
- **AC-005** (REQ-002): in a browser, «hazlo un poco más grande» opens the confirmation «Ahora mide
  80 × 150 mm. Pasará a 100 × 188 mm…», sends nothing until confirmed, then sends `larger`; «más
  grande y con rosas» is sent as a drawing change.
- **AC-006**: lint, typecheck, TS tests, worker ruff/format/mypy/pytest, guard.
