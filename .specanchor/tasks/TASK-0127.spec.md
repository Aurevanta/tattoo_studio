# Task: Enforce the complete Content-Security-Policy, with its reports collected

## 1. Current behaviour (OBSERVED, fb141e3)

TASK-0074 enforced a minimum (`frame-ancestors`, `object-src`, `base-uri`) and sent the complete
policy as Report-Only, with no `report-uri` / `report-to`: nothing collected what it would have
blocked, so it could not be promoted with confidence.

## 2. Desired behaviour (INTENT, owner's audit list 2026-10-16, item 5)

«Completar la política de seguridad CSP. Recoger sus avisos, comprobar las integraciones y activar
restricciones efectivas.»

- **REQ-001** (collect): violations are reported to the studio and recorded for the Panel without
  personal data: directive, blocked origin or keyword, page path without tokens; bounded and
  deduplicated.
- **REQ-002** (check): every page and integration of a production build loads under the policy
  with no violation: fonts, reference thumbnails, the 3D models, the camera page, the service
  worker.
- **REQ-003** (enforce): the complete policy is the enforced `Content-Security-Policy`.

## 3. What the check found

Under the enforced policy, a production build refused `eval` on `/`, `/inicio` and every 3D view,
and the 3D views did not open (0 canvases). Source: Ajv compiling every contract schema with
`new Function` when `@tattoo/contracts` was imported — client components import it for
`BODY_ZONE_SPANS` / `isSleeve`, and the consultation subpaths for the style catalogue. Enforcing
the policy as it stood would have broken the 3D views in production. Fixed at the source:
`contracts` compiles a schema on first `validateAgainst`. Side effect: Ajv is no longer in client
bundles (the 3D harness page's first-load JS fell from 163 kB to 118 kB).
