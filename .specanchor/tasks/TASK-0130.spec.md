# Task: Tattooers accept the stencils on paper — a repeatable acceptance test

## 1. Context

Owner's audit list (2026-10-16, item 9): «Validar las plantillas con tatuadores. Imprimir a escala
y revisar limpieza, detalle y utilidad para transferencia; convertirlo en una prueba de
aceptación.» ADR-0003 says a working tattooer confirming the output is usable on transfer paper is
«the real acceptance test»; `stencil.spec.md` lists the minimum line weight and print acceptance as
NOT_VERIFIED. No such test has been run (OBSERVED: no evidence in the repository).

## 2. Requirements

- **REQ-001** (software): a kit anyone can print, built by the studio's own exporters — no mock-ups —
  with the protocol, an evaluation form and a manifest of hashes that ties a form to its kit.
- **REQ-002** (human): at least three working tattooers run the kit and their forms are recorded in
  `.specanchor/evidence/TASK-0130/` against the criteria below.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

Software (this change):

- **AC-001** (REQ-001): `app/tests/test_acceptance_kit.py` — every sample is the product's export
  at its stated size (first page `(w+20) × (h+35)` mm, its label, `1 + columns × rows` pages), only the
  colour sample carries the approximate note, strokes 0.25 / 0.35 / 0.6, the manifest matches the
  files written.

Human (REQ-002; thresholds are INTENT proposed here, for the owner to confirm before the run):

- **AC-002** Scale: on every print, the 50 mm bar measures 50 ± 0.5 mm and the stated size ± 1 mm.
- **AC-003** Line art (02, 03, 04, 06): at least 2 of 3 tattooers answer «Sí» or «Con retoques
  menores».
- **AC-004** Minimum stroke: the thinnest of 0.25 / 0.35 / 0.6 mm that 2 of 3 call usable becomes the
  stencil spec's minimum line weight; the smallest gap they distinguish after transfer is recorded.
- **AC-005** Colour contours (05): at least 2 of 3 find them useful as a placement guide. If not,
  ADR-0044's deferred alternative (a native line-art pass for colour designs) becomes required.
- **AC-006** Sleeve panels (06): the numbered ticks meet within 2 mm.
